How this board is built (sources, labels, what is modeled)
  • No picks. The board shows matchups and single-value projections with every input line next to them. It does not recommend plays.
  • Sources: ESPN public endpoints — weekly schedule, completed box scores (this season), player game logs (prior seasons, for vs-team meetings only), market line, rosters, and injury designations (ESPN's feed of the NFL's official game statuses). Weather: Open-Meteo hourly forecasts at the stadium. Schemes: FTN charting and nflverse play-by-play/participation data, built weekly into data/schemes.json. Nothing is typed in by hand; a number that can't be sourced is shown as "–" or n=0.
  • Data cutoff: every line uses games completed before the week's first kickoff, so Thursday's game doesn't leak into Sunday's numbers.
  • Rankings 1–32 are per-game averages from box scores. Offense: #1 = most. Defense: #1 = fewest allowed. Points, total yards, rushing yards, net passing yards.
  • Key players = per team, the QB, top 2 RBs, top 3 WRs and top TE by usage in the team's last 5 games (QB dropbacks; RB carries + targets; WR/TE targets). A second TE appears at ≥ 3 targets/game. A player back from injury keeps a slot through his season usage rate and is labelled RETURNING.
  • Projection = weighted blend of three per-game lines. Default 50% last 3 vs this opponent / 25% own last 5 / 25% opponent positional log, adjustable above. A line with n=0 is dropped and the rest renormalized (shown on the card). Samples under full size carry their real n; nothing is padded.
  • Line 1 — own last 5: his last 5 games this season with a recorded dropback, carry or target.
  • Line 2 — opponent positional log: what the opponent allowed to all players at his position in each of its last 5 games. These are measured box-score sums, bucketed by ESPN's listed position (a WR who lines up in the slot is still a WR). To put the team total on his scale, it is multiplied by his share of his own team's output at that position MODELED.
  • Line 3 — last 3 vs this team: 3, not 5, because NFL teams meet rarely. This season plus up to four prior seasons, playoffs included, any club.
  • Injuries: OUT / IR players get no projection and go on the team's Out line. Questionable / doubtful players stay projected, flagged. When an out player held a measurable share of RB carries or team targets in the last 5 games, that volume is redistributed proportionally to the remaining players (×1/(1−share), capped ×1.5) and the card shows base vs adjusted MODELED. A fill-in starting QB's opponent line uses full starter volume. Receivers are not adjusted for a QB change (no measured basis) — the card says so.
  • Schemes MODELED: rates (pass rate over expected, shotgun, play-action, motion, screens, personnel, blitz rate, pass rushers, box count, man/zone, coverage shells) are counted from charting data; identity labels and the front call (3- vs 4-down) are tendency reads, not published playbooks. Fields the source doesn't carry are shown as "not in source".
  • Weather reads are rule-based: wind ≥ 15 mph (gusts ≥ 25) drags on deep passing and kicking, ≥ 20 (gusts ≥ 35) hurts both; rain ≥ 60% or snow favors the run, ball security and short passes; ≤ 32°F mild drag, ≤ 20°F (or feels ≤ 10°F) suppresses scoring. Domes, retractable roofs and SoFi's fixed roof say "Weather N/A (dome)".
  • Projected score MODELED: each side = average of its points/g and the opponent's points allowed/g. The market line (ESPN's listed provider) and the score it implies are shown separately and only when posted.
  • Refresh: the board builds the current week on every load, rolling to next week once all of the current week's games are final. Injuries and forecasts re-pull every 30 minutes while open. The scheme file rebuilds every Wednesday.
